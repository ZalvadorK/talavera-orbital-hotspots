Talavera Orbital: harmonized MODIS–VIIRS hotspot calendar

We are building a web app that shows a burning activity calendar for Mexico and Argentina, harmonized across the MODIS and VIIRS satellite records.

The app, method and validation will be published here before the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 submission deadline.

Data: NASA FIRMS active fire detections (hotspot detections, not individual fires). This project is not endorsed by NASA.
