Counts are satellite hotspot detections, not individual fires. One fire can produce several detections or none.

The CSV has one row per hotspot detection of the harmonized series. Terra and Aqua rows (before 2012) carry a VIIRS-equivalent weight. A download is limited to rows; to get more, narrow the date range or the area.

Method notes

Method notes, validation, limits and sources

Intended use: historical analysis, research and planning. This is not a tactical or real-time response tool. NASA's LANCE disclaimer advises against using these data for tactical decision-making or for conditions at a local scale: LANCE disclaimer.

SI-generated (AI): this app was written by Claude Code during the event and reviewed by the team. The kit used to build it (toolkit, skills, templates) existed before the event and is declared in the repository README.

Data: NASA FIRMS (Fire Information for Resource Management System): MODIS and VIIRS 375 m active-fire detections, distributed through NASA Earthdata and LANCE. Credit: NASA FIRMS.
